## 🌞 What is NovaSolar AI?

**NovaSolar AI** is an AI-powered solar energy intelligence system designed to optimize solar power generation, predict energy output with high accuracy, and enable predictive maintenance — making solar energy smarter, more reliable, and more accessible.

Built at the intersection of **machine learning**, **renewable energy**, and **IoT data science**, NovaSolar AI aims to push the boundaries of what’s possible in solar energy management through cutting-edge forecasting models and research-backed methodologies.

-----

## 🎯 Mission

> To accelerate the global transition to clean energy by making solar systems **smarter**, **self-aware**, and **self-optimizing** through the power of artificial intelligence.

We believe AI is the missing bridge between solar potential and solar performance. NovaSolar AI is that bridge.

-----

## ⚡ Core Features

### 🔮 AI Forecasting Engine

- **Solar irradiance prediction** using deep learning (LSTM, Transformer-based models)
- **Energy output forecasting** — hourly, daily, and weekly horizons
- **Weather-adaptive models** trained on real meteorological datasets
- **Anomaly detection** for unexpected generation drops

### 🛠️ Predictive Maintenance

- Real-time **fault detection** using sensor data analysis
- **Degradation pattern recognition** to extend panel lifespan
- Maintenance scheduling recommendations driven by AI insights
- Alerts system for early-warning failure indicators

### ☀️ Solar Intelligence Dashboard *(In Development)*

- Live monitoring of panel performance metrics
- AI-generated actionable recommendations
- Historical trend analysis and reporting
- Energy yield optimization suggestions

### 📡 Data Pipeline

- Automated dataset collection from weather APIs and IoT sensors
- Preprocessing, normalization, and feature engineering pipeline
- Integration-ready for real solar farm deployments
